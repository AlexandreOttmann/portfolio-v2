## Eledone — AI Platform Engineer

Since **May 2026**, I have been an **AI Platform Engineer** at **Eledone**, a Paris-based startup that automates B2B sales administration. Its AI agents read the orders and quotes that customers send by email, match them against the customers and products in the ERP, and enter them automatically, without changing existing systems.

In practice, this is the task sales admin teams used to repeat by hand all day long: retyping PDF purchase orders into the ERP. Eledone mostly works with distributors and manufacturers that sell from a catalogue.

### 🤖 Automated order entry

- **OCR + LLM → ERP matching** pipeline: purchase orders are extracted (PDFs, emails, scans), matched against customer and product records, then entered into the ERP.
- In production for **dozens of B2B customers**, with **hundreds of orders processed every day**.
- **Python / Django** backend deployed on **Google Cloud Platform**.

### 🏭 Autonomous Software Factory

- Building an autonomous **Software Factory**: AI agents take over part of the team's work.
- **Agentic customer follow-up**: tracking customer requests and changes.
- **Documentation and internal memory**, so the team and the agents always start from the same context.

### 🧩 Platform & processes

- **Modular backend redesign**: better performance and shorter deployments for customers.
- **Process redesign**: onboarding new customers, evolving customer implementations, automating internal tasks.

### 💡 What I'm learning there

Eledone builds on what I was exploring in my personal projects (EONI for RAG, Oni Auction for distributed systems), but in production and at scale: reliable AI agents plugged into critical business systems, with real customers at the other end.

## Software Factory — at Eledone

My main project right now at **Eledone**: building an **autonomous Software Factory**, where AI agents take over part of the team's day-to-day work.

- **Agentic customer follow-up**: customer requests and changes are tracked by agents.
- **Internal task automation**: onboarding, evolving customer implementations, the team's repetitive tasks.
- **Documentation and internal memory**: a shared knowledge base, so the team and the agents start from the same context.

The goal: free the team from repetitive work so it can focus on the product and the customers. More details in the **Eledone** project.
